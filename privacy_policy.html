Painting Wonder – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026
Developer: Sprouts & Spark

Painting Wonder is a colouring and drawing game for young children. We built it so that it needs no personal information at all. This policy explains what that means.

What we collect

Nothing. Painting Wonder does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

The app does not use the internet

The app does not ask for internet permission and does not connect to any server, so nothing you or your child do in the app can be sent anywhere. It does not use the camera, the microphone, contacts, location or any device identifier.

Pictures stay on the device

Pictures that a child saves are kept only in the app's private storage on the device. We cannot see them. You can delete a picture from the gallery inside the app, and uninstalling the app removes all of its saved pictures.

Children's privacy

Painting Wonder is designed for children, including children under 13. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, there is nothing to share with third parties and nothing for a parent to review or remove on our side. The app contains no advertising, no in-app purchases and no links that lead outside the app.

Third-party software

The app is built with the free Godot game engine. It does not include advertising, analytics or social media software.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how the app works in a way that affects privacy, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change reaches the app.
